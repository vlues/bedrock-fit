How Bedrock works

1. Your plan

Home shows today's session, built from your goal, experience, and equipment. Full week under the Plan tab. Weights suggested each set come from what you logged last time — the plan sharpens as you log more.

2. Logging a workout

Tap Start workout, fill in weight + reps per set, add sets if needed, Finish & save. That's the only data entry Bedrock needs to start tracking volume, PRs, and trends automatically. Unsure on form? Tap "Show proper form" under any exercise for a quick cue plus a link to a live video search — Bedrock doesn't guess at a specific video, it points you to search results so you're not stuck with one unverified clip.

3. Progress check-ins

A standing photo + measurements (including hips/thigh now, so glutes/legs focus has real numbers behind it), whenever you want a check-in. The camera shows a live skeleton guide (a real pose-detection model, not a gimmick) to help you frame consistently — it's for alignment only, never for reading body fat, which no phone camera can actually do. If you picked focus areas in onboarding, a "show my focus areas" button overlays your real measurement trends near the relevant joints on your latest photo.

4. Fuel

Supplement education, plus a nutrition tab: daily calorie/protein target, a water tracker, and a food log (type it in, or scan a photo for a rough AI estimate you can edit before saving).

5. Bedrock's AI features (optional)

Add your Anthropic API key in Settings to unlock: a daily insight on Home, data-grounded chat under Ask, photo feedback and trend commentary under Progress, and meal suggestions under Fuel. Everything works without a key too — you just lose the AI layer.

6. Two profiles, one app

Tap your avatar (top-left) to switch profiles or add a second one — each person's history stays separate on their device. If two profiles share a device, Home shows a Household card comparing sessions this week.

7. Where does each number come from?

Bedrock labels this on purpose so you always know what you're looking at: 📊 from your logs means it's computed straight from what you've entered (workouts, check-ins) or synced from Fitbit — pure math, no AI. ⌚ marks anything pulled from a connected wearable. 🤖 marks anything Bedrock generated — commentary, suggestions, estimates — which is always optional and always has a non-AI fallback if you skip the API key.

8. Training load & plateau detection

Progress → Trajectory includes a training-load gauge (acute:chronic workload ratio) — a real sports-science metric used by strength coaches to flag when your volume is climbing faster than your body's adapted to. Separately, if a lift goes 3 sessions with zero weight increase, Bedrock automatically swaps in a fresh exercise for that muscle next session instead of just repeating it — the plan adapts on its own.
